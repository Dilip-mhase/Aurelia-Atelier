Process

A calm commission journey

A simple, guided path from first idea to finished portrait.

  1. 01 Consultation

    We discuss the subject, occasion, size, style, timeline, and the feeling the portrait should carry.

  2. 02 References & Quote

    We review your photos, suggest the best composition, and confirm the quote before reserving a studio slot.

  3. 03 Hand Painting

    The portrait is painted by hand in layered detail using archival materials, with a progress update along the way.

  4. 04 Review & Delivery

    After final approval, the portrait is finished, packed, and delivered with care guidance for display.

Questions

Before you commission

Can you paint from photographs?

Yes. We guide you through reference selection and may request additional images for expression, posture, and color accuracy.

How long does a commission take?

Most portraits take 8 to 14 weeks after concept approval. Larger or multi-subject works may require a longer schedule.

Do you ship finished portraits?

Yes. Finished works can be professionally packed and shipped with tracking and care instructions.
