Studio notes

A quiet studio built around patient observation.

Aurelia Atelier works with a small monthly commission calendar so each portrait receives careful study before it reaches the canvas. Reference photographs are reviewed for light, expression, clothing, gesture, and emotional tone before composition begins.

The studio uses archival canvas, professional pigments, layered underpainting, and protective finishing. Every commission is prepared for long-term display, with guidance on framing, placement, and care.

The artist

Made by Amara Vey, portrait artist and founder.

Amara founded Aurelia Atelier after years of studying classical portrait composition and contemporary family heirloom work. Her process focuses on likeness, restraint, and emotional accuracy: a portrait should feel like the person, not just resemble a photograph.

Clients work directly with the artist from first consultation through final review, keeping the process personal, calm, and highly detailed.
